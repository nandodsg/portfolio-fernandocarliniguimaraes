Hi! I'm Fernando (Nando, if we've ever shared a coffee). A designer by training who turned into a data person, I've been in the field for 12 years, and most recently I led the data and analytics team for the service channels of a Brazilian credit cooperative: strategy, platform, governance, GenAI in production and, above all, people. A multidisciplinary team of analysts, scientists, and data and AI engineers.

To me, leading data means taking that alphabet soup of letters and numbers a production system spits out and turning it into something people can actually read, and above all use to make decisions and know where to go. I never took my hands off the work... the projects below are mine, built with AI agents (yes, I like to understand the mechanism before asking anyone else to do it).

I'm open to talking about my next role in data and AI, leading a team or building hands-on. If that makes sense, reach out!

From innovation to resilience: PIX data in the Rio Grande do Sul floods

May 2024, a historic flood in Rio Grande do Sul. The question sounded simple (who was hit?), but the registered address couldn’t answer it: someone’s home could sit outside the flooded area while their job, their farm or their mom’s house sat right inside it. The turn was to look at the geolocation of PIX transactions (Brazil’s instant payments), find the places where each person actually lives their life, and cross them with the flood map. The first version shipped in under a week.

The model is data scientist Gunnar Guilherme Mayer’s and the scale came from the engineering team; my part was framing the problem, defining what the model had to deliver and getting the result to the people making decisions. And it didn’t end with the flood: it became a company capability, later used for fraud, climate and territorial analysis.
