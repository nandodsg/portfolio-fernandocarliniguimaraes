Hi! I'm Fernando (Nando, if we've ever shared a coffee). A designer by training who turned into a data person, I've been working with data for 12 years, and most recently I led the data and analytics area for the service channels of a Brazilian credit cooperative: strategy, platform, governance, GenAI in production and, above all, people. A mixed team of analysts, scientists, and data and AI engineers.

To me, leading data is taking that soup of letters and numbers and making sure everyone knows where to go. But I never let go of the hands-on part... the projects below are mine, built with AI agents (yes, I like to understand the mechanism before asking anyone else to do it, hehe).

I'm open to my next role in data and AI, leading a team or building hands-on. If that makes sense, reach out!

Open study · Nov 2026 – Present

Brazil's Data Leadership Job Market

Open dataset of data-leadership job postings in Brazil (coordinator × manager), published weekly and automatically from a self-built job-tracking app, with a data contract and confidentiality rules checked before every release.

dbt-on-DuckDB pipeline with tests and CI that needs no database credentials; a site where every number comes from the models, with the SQL that produces it running in the browser; posting lines classified by an LLM chosen by measurement against a hand-reviewed reference set.

A method for building competency matrices from each team's context, for assessment, feedback, merit, promotion and development plans on explicit criteria. It started in the data team I led, was adapted by other leaders and by HR, and became a SaaS built with AI agents.
